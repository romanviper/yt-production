### 2. Audience discovery I intend to create

By the end, the listener should understand the origin of durable numerical recording in Late Uruk as an ecology of overlapping clay practices, not as one clean invention event or a mandatory token → envelope → tablet sequence.

### 3. Intended narrative approach

- **Intended opening:** begin with the physical problem of information being present but not directly visible when counters are enclosed in a clay envelope. Basis: `HS-P01-0003`.
- **Central narrative spine/question:** follow the change in *where numerical information physically sits and how directly it can be inspected* as clay practices coexist. Basis: overlay `historical_change`; `HS-P01-0001`; `HS-P01-0007`.
- **Intended ending/destination:** land on direct marks on durable clay surfaces as a consequential shift without presenting them as an immediate replacement for earlier devices. Basis: `HS-P01-0004`; `HS-P01-0007`; overlay `audience_discovery`.
- **Narrator stance:** close observational historical narration, emphasizing objects and affordances rather than named actors or speculative institutions. Basis: substrate primitives omit named participants and transaction mechanisms.

### 4. Evidence I expect to rely on

- `overlay.historical_territory` — limits P01 to Late-Uruk clay recording/authentication practices and the move toward direct surface recording.
- `overlay.audience_discovery` — target realization: overlapping ecology rather than linear genealogy.
- `HS-P01-0001` — several clay-based recording/authentication practices coexisted.
- `HS-P01-0003` — envelopes could enclose counters; enclosed counters were not directly inspectable while closed; some examples carried exterior impressions/sealings.
- `HS-P01-0004` — numerical tablets could place quantity information directly on a durable exterior surface; some examples also carried authentication marks.
- `HS-P01-0007` — qualified historical change toward more direct surface recording while older/parallel practices continued.

### 5. Constraints I am actively carrying

- Do not convert overlapping practices into a mandatory token → envelope → tablet developmental chain.
- Do not state or imply wholesale replacement of envelopes/tokens by tablets.
- Do not invent a specific market, tax, tribute, redistribution, labor-obligation, ownership, commodity, transaction, or named-actor mechanism.
- Exterior impressions/sealings apply only to some envelopes/tablets, not universally.
- Keep the prose concrete enough to work as a historical-podcast excerpt while remaining inside the sparse approved substrate.
