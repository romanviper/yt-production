# Modular Historical Narrative Workflow

## 1. Mental model

Repo separates **decision ownership**, nhưng creative ownership phải nối liền từ question tới prose; không để story rơi vào khoảng trống giữa Research, Outline và Writer.

| Layer | Owns | Does not own |
|---|---|---|
| Research | truth, source/locator, chronology, uncertainty, contradiction **và discovery of narratable situations/processes/relationships** | final prose; cố chứng minh một thesis đã khóa trước |
| Outline | central question, selected journey, structural cases/relationships, movement order, dependency, information placement, ending accumulation | sentence-level prose, local cadence |
| Writer | execution inside architecture + evidence boundary; local fact selection, pacing, scale, POV, imagery, transitions | silently expanding truth ceiling; final approval |
| Review | product-first judgment + failure diagnosis + separate integrity check | enforcing one universal storytelling recipe |
| Harness | authority, write scope, lifecycle, provenance, integrity, resource limits | choosing the story for the creative agents |

Human/Owner retains final content approval.

## 2. Canonical production path

```text
Owner question / bounded work order
  → editorial question + provisional scope
  → exploratory research
  → compare 2–3 whole-work routes
  → select route from story strength + source substance
  → targeted research across opening, middle and ending
  → whole-work outline
  → narrative compression
  → read compression as product
  → repair the responsible layer
  → evidence/integrity check
  → Owner review
  → full prose only if separately authorized
```

A direct Owner instruction can authorize this whole bounded chain. Do not recreate legacy P01/story-plan approval gates when the order explicitly covers the whole pass.

## 3. Research: truth + discovery

Research has two coupled jobs:

1. determine what can be said honestly;
2. discover what can carry a story.

Preserve source URL/locator, provenance, chronology, causal confidence, uncertainty and counterevidence. Also preserve the **content of the historical situation**, not only the scholar's conclusion: who is involved, what is happening, pressures/obstacles, sequence, consequence, surviving words/objects/places and what remains unknown.

Claims are not a narratability quota. A large ledger does not prove a movement has enough substance. If a case cannot support an actual development, reduce its role, deepen research or replace it.

Documented traditions, hypotheses and guided inference are legitimate research material when epistemic status remains visible. Reconstruction may support continuity/experience but cannot establish a historical conclusion.

## 4. Route selection

A topic is not yet a story. Before committing the architecture, compare a small number of materially different whole-work possibilities. For each, identify what the listener follows, what changes, what concrete material can sustain it, how it differs from a taxonomy of functions/attributes, and where evidence is weak.

The central question is a search instrument, not a verdict that all evidence must confirm. Research may change it.

## 5. Outline: architecture of the journey

The outline owns consequential decisions before prose:

- question, promise and scope;
- opening that makes something worth following;
- historical movements/cases/processes selected for the whole journey;
- why each movement exists and why the order matters;
- dependencies and transitions;
- where exposition is needed and what problem it solves;
- how the middle accumulates weight for the ending;
- material/source refs and boundaries that constrain the route;
- flexible duration estimates.

Outline may choose a recurring relationship, case, investigation, discovery order or structural point of view when that choice is essential. It should not prewrite every paragraph/camera beat.

Production units such as `P##` are context/revision units, not a chapter taxonomy. Do not infer sections from a list of capabilities/functions of the subject.

## 6. Writer authorship

Writer receives the selected architecture and the material needed to make it real. Writer tells a world happening or being discovered; explanation appears where it helps the listener continue something already made worth understanding.

Writer owns local execution: selection among permitted detail, pacing, imagery, sentence craft, transitions, local POV/scale and how to move between scene, summary, exposition, text/object and inference.

No universal protagonist/antagonist/hook/second-person/climax formula applies. Long exposition is legitimate when earned. If the architecture promises a movement that the supplied material cannot carry, Writer should reopen/flag the responsible layer rather than conceal the weakness with cinematic language.

## 7. Whole-work compression

Compression is a **story told briefly**, not a synopsis of topic headings. It must let the Owner directly experience an opening, developing middle and ending. Preserve enough circumstances, events/processes, discoveries, relationships and consequences that the route remains intelligible and cumulative.

Reduce extent/detail; do not reduce every movement to one conclusion sentence. Historical limits should enter naturally where they change interpretation. No camera directions, scorecards or meta explanations belong in the artifact.

For `sumer-writing` reset, canonical output is:

`writer-output/full-script/narrative-identity-reset-01/compression.md`

## 8. Review and repair

Read the artifact first, then compare it with intent and evidence. Ask what the listener has to follow, what changes, whether later movements develop earlier ones, whether the ending is earned and whether the human consequence appears through events/relations rather than narrator claims.

Then perform historical integrity checks separately.

Route failures to the responsible layer:

- weak central question/journey → brief/story choice;
- interchangeable middle → architecture;
- case lacks event/process/consequence → research;
- useful source detail disappeared in transfer → handoff;
- adequate route/material but weak telling → prose;
- chronology/causal/epistemic overreach → historical integrity.

Do not change rubrics to bless an unchanged output and do not run endless evaluator loops.

## 9. Runtime and legacy compatibility

Hard infrastructure may continue to enforce authority, write paths, freshness, provenance and evidence ceilings. Existing task/materialize/section tooling remains available for products or later full-prose production when actually needed.

Legacy story-plan, P01-only and eight-section artifacts remain as historical/compatibility evidence. They do **not** override the canonical creative path above. Code should only be changed when a real runtime dependency forces the new architecture back into the old one.

For current `sumer-writing`, `products/sumer-writing/02_outline/outline.md` is the canonical narrative architecture pending Owner review. A machine mirror may be synchronized when runtime compatibility requires it; old approval metadata must not be inherited.
