# Operation — Design Section

## Responsibility

Turn the section brief and evidence pool into a compact design decision. Do not write narration and do not pre-script the writer's route.

## Output intent

`story-plan.json` records only:

- the audience shift this work unit must achieve;
- a concise, free-form story strategy explaining why the selected material can create that shift;
- evidence roles: `core`, `optional`, `guardrail` and `exclude`;
- a recommended length range with a short material-based rationale.

Partition every available claim exactly once so the evidence ceiling is explicit. Selection is a boundary decision, not a demand that every selected claim be spoken.

The strategy may describe a promising approach, tension or hinge, but it must not prescribe numbered beats, paragraph count, cadence, a compulsory opening device or a miniature payoff formula. Those remain writer decisions.

Status remains `draft`. Only the user approves the plan. If the material cannot create the requested audience shift, report whether the problem belongs to evidence, the local section boundary or the whole-product architecture.
