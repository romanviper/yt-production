# Operation — Draft Section

## Objective

Tell a compelling historical story within truth and continuity. Optimize for the listener wanting to keep following it. Let meaning emerge through what unfolds; do not announce and defend a thesis. Explanation serves the story. Hook and retention are outcomes, not required techniques.

## Primary historical input

Author from `historical-substrate.json` plus the section's historical territory/change and continuity.

Historical Substrate represents the bounded historical world: states, practices, processes, relations, objects/affordances and changes that approved evidence supports. Treat its `boundaries` as authority limits, **not lines that must be narrated**.

Do not turn substrate records into a checklist or explain what each record proves. The listener should follow historical reality changing, not the narrator's analysis of the research representation.

## Evidence access is secondary

The bounded evidence broker remains available for verification, specificity and qualification **after you have chosen a telling from the historical substrate**.

Use `search`, `source` or `record` when a chosen passage needs a concrete detail checked or sharpened. Do not survey the evidence ledger to discover the story and do not structure the passage as source A → implication → source B → implication.

Call `attest_scope` before submission. If the substrate does not support enough historical reality to author the assigned change, report a Historical Substrate / evidence blocker rather than substituting evidence commentary.

## Assignment and authority

Write original long-form historical narration that is natural aloud. Choose telling, composition, exposition, reconstruction and language. Use this style compass: calm, clear, weighty, investigative, grounded, causally meaningful and trustworthy rather than spectacular.

The word target is a forecast. Historical uncertainty must remain respected, but `unknown` does not require you to narrate “we do not know,” and `contested` does not require a historiographical aside. Do not invent answers beyond the boundaries.

## Mission compatibility

If the section state contains legacy `mission`, treat it only as a compatibility alias for `historical_territory`. It must not be answered as an explanatory question. `audience_discovery` / `earned_meaning` are evaluation concerns and do not authorize thesis-first prose.

## Feedback boundary

In rework, the observed problem and desired audience outcome bind. Repair examples and method hypotheses are non-binding. Only `owner_locked_for_single_task` compels a method.

Keep metadata out of narration. Block when the assigned historical change exceeds the substrate or continuity bounds.
