# Operation — Draft Section

## Responsibility

Write one section that achieves the approved audience shift and advances its assigned act/movement. Treat upstream design as intent, evidence selection and story movement, not prose to be restated. The Agent chooses ordering, opening form, paragraph count, rhythm and transitions.

The writer's job is not to explain the design artifacts in fluent prose. Make the selected material produce the experience. Prefer:

**material → action/change → consequence → only the explanation still needed**

over alternating material with repeated interpretation or summary.

## Narrative stance

Place the audience beside the evidence rather than beneath a lecture. When material allows, let them encounter an object, action, process, consequence, contrast or change before explanation. Narrator clarification should sharpen an inference already underway, not perform the full `observe → interpret → qualify → conclude` sequence for them.

After a concrete sequence has made a relation clear, explain only what the sequence cannot safely carry by itself: necessary identification, evidence boundary, causal qualification or a genuinely new consequence. Do not restate the same landed meaning in progressively more abstract wording.

Use a deletion test on explanatory sentences: if removing a sentence leaves the listener with the same understanding, evidence boundary and consequence, the sentence is probably redundant. In particular, avoid `show X → explain X → paraphrase X → summarize X`.

Do not stage specialist classification as audience observation. If a cue only becomes meaningful after expert identification, give that identification as narrator context instead of implying the listener could already see its meaning for themselves.

A real object, documented action, process, failure, transformation or bounded puzzle can carry story without invented drama. A section does not need a historical event chain if another carrier creates genuine narrative movement. Compress stretches that mainly deliver processed analysis, or return to the concrete carrier.

Core evidence anchors the draft. Optional evidence appears only when useful. Guardrails are silent constraints by default: they prevent overclaiming without automatically becoming narration. State a guardrail only when omitting it would create a material audience misconception, and use the shortest wording that protects the boundary. Excluded claims stay out. Preserve approved entry/exit state and continuity.

Prefer consequences of the material over narrator commentary about the audience's own interpretation. Phrases such as “điều đáng chú ý”, “bây giờ câu hỏi trở thành”, “chính ở đây chúng ta thấy” or “đừng vội nghĩ” are not forbidden, but repeated use is a warning that the narrator may be describing the story instead of letting it happen.

Do not turn a narrative implication, intuitive consequence or useful metaphor into a substantive historical function or causal claim unless the narration pack supports it. If the approved shift requires such a claim, or depends on evidence the audience cannot actually perceive as designed, report the mismatch for design/evidence routing instead of concealing it with prose.

## Self-edit before handoff

Draft freely first. Before submitting, make a separate economy pass and remove or compress:

- an idea that already landed once;
- explanation after the material already made the same relation clear;
- guardrails that became methodology exposition;
- abstract commentary that does not add a needed fact, boundary or consequence;
- narrator language that mainly tells the audience what they are now thinking;
- explanation that continues after the section payoff has already landed.

Do not optimize for shortness or a percentage cut. Keep a sentence when it materially advances the event/state, sharpens concrete reconstruction, supplies necessary information or qualification, or creates a new consequence. Otherwise test whether the section is stronger without it.

Length is an estimate, never a quota. Do not pad.

Write natural spoken Vietnamese and a compact handoff. Do not review or approve your own draft.
