# Operation — Draft Section

## Responsibility

Write one section that achieves the approved audience shift and advances its act/movement. Upstream design defines intent, evidence and story movement, not prose to paraphrase. The writer chooses ordering, opening, rhythm and transitions.

Make the selected material produce the experience. Prefer:

**material → action/change → consequence → only the explanation still needed**

Place the audience beside the evidence, not beneath a lecture. After a concrete sequence makes a relation clear, add only what it cannot safely carry itself: necessary identification, evidence boundary, causal qualification or a new consequence.

Use a deletion test: if removing an explanatory sentence preserves the same understanding, evidence limit and consequence, cut or compress it. Avoid `show X → explain X → paraphrase X → summarize X`.

Do not present specialist classification as audience observation. Supply expert identification plainly when needed, then let the audience follow what changes because of it.

Guardrails are silent constraints by default. Speak one only when omission would create a material misconception, and use the shortest wording that protects the boundary. Excluded claims stay out.

Objects, actions, processes, failures, transformations, consequences, contrasts or bounded puzzles may carry story; no event chain is mandatory. Repeated meta-commentary about what is “important”, what “we now see” or how the question “has changed” is a warning that narration is explaining the story instead of letting it happen.

Stay inside the narration pack. If the required movement needs unsupported evidence or cannot work as designed, report the blocker instead of compensating with prose.

## Self-edit before handoff

Draft freely first. Then make one economy pass for repeated ideas, explanation after a relation has already landed, guardrail exposition, meta-commentary and explanation after the payoff. Do not target a percentage cut. Keep sentences that advance state, sharpen concrete reconstruction, add necessary information/qualification or create a new consequence.

Length is an estimate, not a quota. Do not pad. Write natural spoken Vietnamese and a compact handoff. Do not review or approve your own draft.
