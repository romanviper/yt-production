# Public Audience Source Profile — 2026-08-25

Operator-only calibration source. This file records public observations about a named comparison audience so an anonymous runtime projection can be maintained. It must never enter a writer, reviewer or target-viewer packet, and it grants no authority to imitate another channel's prose, voice, motifs, pacing or structure.

## Provenance and scope

The quantitative observations below came from public vidIQ channel and video surfaces retrieved on 2026-08-25. The qualitative observations came from relevance-sorted public comments. No comment is reproduced verbatim.

This is a calibration snapshot, not private audience analytics and not a claim that public commenters represent all viewers.

### Public-comment sample

The qualitative sample contained 220 relevance-sorted public comment threads across the Sumerians, Majapahit and Assyrians videos, plus 96 recent channel-level public threads. Repeated themes included:

- appreciation for unusually long historical treatments;
- audio-first use, replaying, relaxed listening and listening around sleep;
- preference for calm human narration and restrained rather than intrusive music;
- reliance on mental imagery, maps and useful visuals for orientation;
- story, emotion, transport and a felt connection to vanished worlds;
- accuracy, depth, nuance and willingness to discuss uncertainty;
- local or ancestral identity and recognition;
- resistance to sensationalism and synthetic-feeling presentation.

These themes establish presence, not population frequency. The comments are nonrandom, self-selected observations; relevance sorting and the absence of a random sampling frame prevent prevalence estimates.

## Inferred

The following are operator hypotheses derived from the observations, not measured viewer attributes:

- A useful target-viewer simulation should tolerate patient development and should not mistake low arousal for disengagement.
- The listener may be audio-first, but sustained causal coherence and a concrete mental scene still matter; background listenability is not a substitute for comprehension.
- Curiosity is likely to work best as a slow sequence of credible questions and payoffs rather than as repeated high-intensity hooks.
- Trust may depend on historical depth, proportionate uncertainty and freedom from sensationalism.
- Lived consequence and spatial or material orientation may help an unfamiliar civilization feel emotionally present without invented scenes.
- Re-listenability may reward layered clarity, but public data cannot show whether a particular passage caused replay, sleep use or completion.

## Unknown

The public snapshot does not establish:

- viewer demographics, education, geography or language proficiency;
- impressions, click-through rate, audience-retention curves, completion rate or exact abandonment points;
- subscriber versus non-subscriber behavior;
- whether relaxed or sleep-adjacent listening represents attention, habit, comfort or passive playback;
- the prevalence of any comment theme across silent viewers;
- causal links between narration, visuals, music, topic choice, length and performance;
- private channel analytics or any individual viewer's behavior.
