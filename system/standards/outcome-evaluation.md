# Outcome Evaluation Standard

Judge the draft by **outcome → diagnosis**, never method compliance.

## Primary judgment

First evaluate:

- section objective and exit state;
- meaningful listener progression;
- authored narration rather than research recital;
- experience/world/process/relationship formation when appropriate;
- spoken-listening quality;
- causal clarity;
- continuity;
- evidence integrity: no invention, false certainty, truth-ceiling expansion or hidden qualification/contradiction.

A draft may pass through many routes. Do not fail it for lacking a carrier, before/after, raw clue, process sequence or recount-before-interpret structure.

## Diagnose after failure

Only after an outcome problem is observed, use mechanics as optional diagnostics: concrete focus, delayed interpretation, contrast/before-after, process sequence, scale shift, or cutting explanatory echo/meta-narration. These suggest repairs; they are not invariants.

## Conditional audience agency

Pseudo-agency matters only when narration stages audience discovery/inference. Then the audience must have had access to the relevant evidence before specialist classification. Straight expert narration is valid.

## Common failures

- `document mode`: accurate information without an authored listening progression;
- explanatory echo/meta-narration overload;
- causal blur;
- evidence overreach.

## Routing

- `prose_execution`: objective/evidence territory are sound; execution/listening failed.
- `product_architecture`: objective, boundary, progression or evidence territory is wrong.
- `evidence`: support is missing/contradicted/insufficient or requires truth-ceiling expansion.

No active story-plan/local-design authority exists on the current path. Give the smallest responsible revision scope and an observable acceptance test.
