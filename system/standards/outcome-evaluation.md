# Outcome Evaluation Standard

Evaluate the draft that exists, not whether it followed one preferred route.

## What to judge

- **Arc contribution:** does the section advance its assigned opening/body/ending role?
- **Narrative movement:** does story or understanding change, or does prose mainly restate information?
- **Material carries motion:** do objects, actions, processes, contrasts, failures or consequences carry most movement, with narration adding only what material cannot safely make clear?
- **Audience agency:** does the listener encounter evidence/change and infer something, or receive nearly every observation already processed?
- **Audience readability:** could a non-specialist notice the raw clue before specialist classification? Separate evidence present, perceivable and interpretable.
- **Narrator stance:** guide/recount/clarify, or repeatedly tell the audience what to see and think?
- **Causal clarity:** can the listener follow pressure → mechanism → consequence where relevant?
- **Voice continuity:** concrete, calm, investigative, natural spoken Vietnamese?
- **Scale control:** are shifts from object/person to institution/system earned?
- **Semantic economy:** does each major idea land once rather than being shown, interpreted, paraphrased and summarized again?
- **Guardrail discipline:** are evidence limits mostly silent constraints? Spoken guardrails should prevent a material misconception, not recite every research caveat.
- **Evidence integrity:** no invention, unsupported function/causation, false certainty or defensive disclaimer pile.
- **Listening experience:** without visual annotation, can the listener follow what is encountered or changing and state each paragraph's new contribution plainly?

## Failure patterns

Flag document-mode when a stretch is dominated by narrator-run `observe → interpret → qualify → conclude`. Accuracy does not make this storytelling.

Flag explanatory echo when the draft does `show X → explain X → paraphrase X → summarize X` without a new fact, boundary or consequence. Deletion test: if removing a sentence preserves the same understanding, evidence limit and consequence, it is likely redundant. Do not impose a numeric cut target.

Repeated meta-narration such as “điều đáng chú ý”, “bây giờ câu hỏi trở thành” or “chính ở đây chúng ta thấy” is a warning, not an automatic violation. Flag it when narration describes how the audience should process the story instead of letting material/consequence produce the shift.

Flag pseudo-agency when expert classification is supplied first and the resulting inference is then staged as an audience discovery. Remove the specialist classification: can a casual listener still identify the raw clue?

Do not require invented drama or a historical event chain as the fix. An object, person, action, process, consequence, contrast, failure or bounded puzzle can carry movement if the audience can follow what changes.

## Diagnose the layer

Classify each material failure as:

- `prose_execution`: intent/evidence are sound; wording, pacing, arrangement, redundancy or narrator stance failed.
- `local_design`: audience shift, carrier or evidence selection cannot produce the needed experience.
- `product_architecture`: section boundary or whole-script movement design is wrong.
- `evidence`: needed claim/material is missing, contradicted, too uncertain or insufficient.

Route to the smallest responsible layer and give an observable acceptance test. Do not create a global writer rule for a one-off failure.
