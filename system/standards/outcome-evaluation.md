# Outcome Evaluation Standard

Evaluate the draft that exists, not whether it followed one preferred route.

## What to judge

- **Arc contribution:** does the section advance the opening, body or ending role assigned by the product blueprint?
- **Narrative movement:** does understanding or story state change, or does the prose only restate information?
- **Material carries motion:** do objects, actions, processes, contrasts, failures or consequences carry most of the movement, with narration adding only what the material cannot safely make clear by itself? Flag stretches where the narrator repeatedly stops the material to restate its meaning.
- **Audience agency:** does the listener get to encounter evidence, action, consequence or change and do some noticing or inference, or does the narrator pre-process nearly every observation into a conclusion for them?
- **Audience readability:** when the draft asks the audience to infer from a clue, could a non-specialist actually notice the relevant raw cue without first being given the specialist classification that makes it meaningful? Distinguish evidence being present from evidence being perceivable and interpretable.
- **Narrator stance:** does the narrator guide, recount and clarify, or drift into a teacher/analyst role that continuously explains what the audience should see, think or notice about the story itself?
- **Causal clarity:** can the listener follow pressure → mechanism → consequence where that relation is relevant?
- **Voice continuity:** does the narration remain concrete, calm, investigative and natural in spoken Vietnamese?
- **Scale control:** does it move between object/person, institution and system only when the explanation earns that change?
- **Semantic economy:** does each major idea land once, or is it shown, interpreted, paraphrased and summarized again after the listener already has it?
- **Guardrail discipline:** are evidence limits mostly doing silent constraint work, or have they leaked into methodology exposition? A spoken guardrail should exist because omitting it would create a material misconception, not merely because the research contains a caveat.
- **Evidence integrity:** are claims, uncertainty and exclusions handled without invention or defensive disclaimer piles? Flag functional, causal or memory/social claims that go beyond the narration pack even when they sound like intuitive consequences of the object.
- **Listening experience:** without relying on visual annotation to perform the reasoning, can the listener follow what is being encountered or changing, and can each paragraph's new contribution be paraphrased in one ordinary sentence?

## Document-mode, redundancy and pseudo-agency failure

Flag a material failure when a stretch is dominated by `observe → interpret → qualify → conclude` performed by the narrator while the audience receives already-processed analysis. This can be accurate and coherent and still fail as storytelling.

Also flag explanatory echo when the draft effectively does `show X → explain X → paraphrase X → summarize X` without adding a new fact, evidence boundary or consequence. Use a deletion test: if removing an explanatory sentence preserves the same understanding, evidence limit and consequence, the sentence is likely redundant. Do not require a numeric cut target; diagnose the actual repeated function.

Repeated meta-narration such as “điều đáng chú ý”, “bây giờ câu hỏi trở thành”, “chính ở đây chúng ta thấy” or “đừng vội nghĩ” is a warning sign, not an automatic violation. Flag it when the narrator is describing how the audience should process the story instead of allowing the material or consequence to produce that shift.

Also flag pseudo-agency when the draft first supplies an expert category or decoded meaning and then treats the resulting inference as something the audience independently discovered. The acceptance test is simple: remove the narrator's specialist classification; can a casual listener still identify the raw clue they are supposedly noticing?

Do not require invented drama or a mandatory historical event chain as the fix. A concrete object, person, action, process, consequence, contrast, failure or bounded puzzle may carry genuine narrative movement if the audience can actually follow what changes.

## Diagnose the layer before prescribing a fix

Classify every material failure as one of:

- `prose_execution`: the intent and evidence are sound; wording, pacing, arrangement, redundancy or narrator stance failed.
- `local_design`: the intended audience shift, carrier or evidence selection cannot produce the needed experience or movement.
- `product_architecture`: the section boundary or whole-script act/movement design is wrong.
- `evidence`: the needed claim, event, object or documented action is missing, contradicted, too uncertain or insufficient for the inference being made.

Do not add a new writer rule for a one-off failure. Route the issue to the smallest responsible layer and give an observable acceptance test.
