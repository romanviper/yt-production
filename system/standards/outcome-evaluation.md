# Outcome Evaluation Standard

Judge the produced work first, then diagnose the layer that caused any failure. Do not substitute method compliance, qualifications or stylistic markers for the listening experience.

## Whole-work questions

1. After the opening, what concrete historical reality, relationship or question does a willing listener have reason to follow?
2. What changes or is discovered through the middle, and does each later movement develop, complicate or transform something established earlier?
3. Are exposition and contextual explanation arriving because they help the listener continue that journey, rather than because a taxonomy says the topic must be covered?
4. Does the human consequence of writing appear through circumstances, actions, relationships and outcomes rather than narrator assertions alone?
5. Would removing or freely swapping major movements leave the experience unchanged? If so, diagnose a likely architecture problem unless montage/juxtaposition has a clear whole-work function.
6. Does the ending have weight accumulated by the body rather than merely restating the opening image or thesis?
7. Does writing remain causally or relationally central rather than becoming an inserted theme inside a general history?

## Historical integrity

Separately check chronology, provenance, representativeness, causal scope and epistemic status. Fail fabricated documentary fact, hidden qualification or a tradition/hypothesis/reconstruction promoted to fact. Do not fail honest uncertainty simply because it prevents a clean verdict.

## Diagnosis and routing

Route the smallest responsible repair:

- `story_choice / brief`: the central question or selected journey cannot sustain the work;
- `architecture`: the middle is interchangeable, movements do not accumulate, or boundaries/order are wrong;
- `evidence / research`: a promised event/process/consequence lacks support or source-level substance;
- `handoff`: research contains useful material that outline/Writer did not receive;
- `prose_execution`: route and material are adequate but the telling itself loses orientation, momentum or clarity;
- `historical_integrity`: claim, chronology, causality or epistemic status exceeds sources.

Describe the observed failure and desired outcome. Do not prescribe a universal storytelling device. Owner retains final quality judgment.
