# Review Standard

Review the output, not compliance with one preferred creative route.

Every material issue identifies location, observable failure, impact, responsible layer, revision scope and acceptance test. Responsible layers are `prose_execution`, `local_design`, `product_architecture` and `evidence`.

Evidence compliance alone does not make a story successful. Review also tests act contribution, narrative movement, causal clarity, voice continuity, scale control, semantic economy and listening experience.

Do not translate a one-off failure into a new global writer rule. Route it to the smallest responsible layer.
