# Voice Evaluation Reference

The Channel Constitution is the primary voice instruction. This file helps evaluators diagnose consistency after prose exists; it is not loaded into writer packets.

## Stable identity

The narrator is calm, clear, weighty and investigative. They guide the audience through a concrete problem instead of performing expertise. Causality outranks chronology, consequence outranks trivia and people remain visible inside systems.

## Adaptive expression

No fixed sentence pattern, paragraph rhythm, opening motif or transition belongs to the channel. The writer may begin with an object, person, action or situation and may change scale whenever the mechanism earns it.

Voice consistency means that two valid drafts feel related in judgement and attitude, not mechanically identical in cadence.

## Evaluation cues

- Concrete nouns and active subjects carry the explanation.
- Abstract terms appear only when ordinary Vietnamese cannot stay precise.
- Uncertainty is brief and changes the conclusion; it does not become defensive exposition.
- Emotional weight comes from evidenced consequence.
- Scale moves from the tangible to institution/system and back to human effect with a clear reason.
- Benchmark learning remains functional; surface wording, cadence, motifs, persona and signature structure remain original.

A product voice profile may narrow distance, emotional register and benchmark-derived functions, but it cannot override the Channel Constitution.
