# Creative Boundaries

These are the only content boundaries the creative Agent must carry:

1. Stay inside the section's approved evidence ceiling and continuity scope.
2. Choose the form that best carries the passage: direct exposition, a concrete image, comparison, scene or a blend. Do not force a scene, character or point of view when the evidence supports exposition better.
3. A generalized illustration may embody already-supported practices and consequences, but it must not add specific people, speech, private thought, motive, date, measurement, causal event or outcome and must not pose as one documented occurrence.
4. Keep uncertainty inside ordinary narrative phrasing. Never expose claim IDs, the evidence contract, a reconstruction disclaimer or a list of things that were not invented.
5. Do not imitate a reference creator's wording, cadence, motifs, narrator persona or signature structure.
6. If a useful move needs missing evidence or conflicts with the section boundary, report the blocker instead of hiding it with prose.

Within those boundaries, emphasis, omission, structure, pacing and voice belong to the writer.
