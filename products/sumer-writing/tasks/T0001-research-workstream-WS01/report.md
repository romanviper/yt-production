## Key result

The evidence supports an early Uruk/southern Mesopotamian proto-cuneiform tradition around 3350/3300–3000 BCE, but not the unqualified sentence “the Sumerians invented writing in 3200 BCE.” Definition, exact date, underlying language, ethnic attribution and world priority remain distinct questions.
