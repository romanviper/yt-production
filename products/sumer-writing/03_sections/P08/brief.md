# P08 — Sống lâu bằng cách trở thành thứ khác

## Narrative job

Explain spread through adaptation; separate script, language and institution.

## Entry state

Khán giả equates survival with unchanged Sumerian language/culture.

## Exit state

Khán giả tracks cuneiform script, learned Sumerian and reproducing institutions separately.

## Question

Làm sao tradition sống khi ngôn ngữ và state đầu tiên thay đổi?

## Payoff

Survival comes from costly adaptation through specialists, courts, diplomacy and curricula.

## Anchor requirements

- Akkadian adoption as primary transformation.
- Hittite selective-transfer case.

## Bridge in

Infrastructure becomes portable through carriers and change.

## Bridge out

Dependence on costly reproduction creates vulnerability.

## Boundary

Không write global history of every cuneiform language.

## Risk

Name carrier and transformation cost for spread claims.
