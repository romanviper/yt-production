# P04 — Một hệ thống học cách ghi ngôn ngữ

## Narrative job

Theo dõi medium, layout, sign values and language without alphabet teleology.

## Entry state

Khán giả đồng nhất earliest marks với mature wedge-shaped language-complete cuneiform.

## Exit state

Khán giả tách medium, ductus, layout, script and language; capacity expands gradually and stays logo-syllabic.

## Question

Tablet đi từ giữ quantities/categories tới names, clauses and languages thế nào?

## Payoff

Accumulated graphic, metrological and phonetic adaptations—not a march to alphabet.

## Anchor requirements

- Contrast incised proto-cuneiform with later wedges.
- One layout and one repeated phonetic/logo-syllabic example.

## Bridge in

Institutional demand selected an initially bounded system.

## Bridge out

Capacity is not use; next examine actual uptake.

## Boundary

Không catalogue every sign stage or imply alphabet endpoint.

## Risk

Technical detail must change audience model of capability.
