# P03 — Khi quy mô vượt quá trí nhớ cục bộ

## Narrative job

Đặt institutional scale as major selection pressure; separate accounting, exchange, redistribution, labor, obligation and ownership.

## Entry state

Khán giả biết tools có sẵn nhưng chưa biết vì sao system consolidated.

## Exit state

Khán giả hiểu stable records were selected by classification/coordination problems; evidence supports co-development, not one-way causation.

## Question

Điều gì trong đời sống đô thị khiến durable records đáng trả chi phí?

## Payoff

Institutions create demand for stable classifications: forward arrow of feedback, not yet return arrow.

## Anchor requirements

- Uruk IV–III corpus as qualitative evidence; no uncited percentage.
- Mechanism matrix for transfer, redistribution, labor, obligation, ownership, exchange.

## Bridge in

Many tools become a system when coordination rewards standardization.

## Bridge out

Pressure explains selection; next inspect technical state.

## Boundary

Không chứng minh record đã làm institution powerful; return arrow ở P07.

## Risk
