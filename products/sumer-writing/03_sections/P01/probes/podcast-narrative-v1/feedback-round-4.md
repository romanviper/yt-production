## Diagnosis of the review loop

### 1. Relative-ranking bias replaced the absolute product bar

The Reviewer repeatedly identified the strongest output in the current batch and described its local gains. It did not first ask whether any output crossed a minimum high-quality podcast threshold.

A “best of four” result may still be a clear product failure. Future reviews must report both relative rank and absolute status, with absolute status controlling the decision.

### 2. Confirmation bias accumulated around an unverified causal theory

The review sequence constructed a chain of proposed missing layers:

```text
rich material
→ Writer-owned composition
→ operation-level composition
→ material carrier
```

Each later review treated the previous hypothesis as partly validated and searched for the next missing layer. The experiments did not support that causal confidence:

- prompt changes were not single-variable;
- clean input and one-pass execution were not auditable;
- no blind product evaluation was run;
- no listener comparison established convergence toward the benchmark.

The carrier recommendation was an editorial hypothesis, not a demonstrated solution. Round 4 shows that adding an object requirement can produce a late evidentiary example without creating a strong narrative.

### 3. The process is not an editorial feedback loop

The current flow is:

```text
Reviewer diagnosis
→ diagnosis compiled into additional Writer-task constraints
→ fresh Writer cannot see the previous draft or feedback
→ new output generated from scratch
```

The Writer never revises its own work in response to an editor. The system is repeatedly sampling from an increasingly constrained prompt, not performing editorial convergence.

Fresh-Writer isolation may serve a bounded causal experiment. It should not be mistaken for the best production process when the owner is asking for maximum output quality.

### 4. Proxy optimization displaced the actual listening experience

The loop increasingly rewarded measurable proxies:

- word-count compliance;
- one-practice scope;
- physical verbs and visible state changes;
- presence of a material carrier;
- an empty `causal_bridges_claimed` field;
- explicit truth qualifiers.

These are useful constraints or diagnostics. They are not sufficient evidence of a high-quality history podcast.

Round 4 demonstrates the resulting Goodhart effect. The Writer task grew by roughly 31 percent and included issue-by-issue evaluator language. The output then echoed phrases such as physical asymmetry, non-evolutionary progression, and material logic. The prose became better at displaying compliance than at creating an authored listening experience.

### 5. Product editing and truth auditing were collapsed into one role

Historical-integrity findings in the prior reviews were often valid. The unsupported step was assuming that each truth repair, or the next composition constraint introduced to preserve energy, also moved the product closer to the podcast benchmark.

The two questions must be evaluated independently and in sequence:

1. Does the output work as the requested listening product?
2. If it does, which claims or reconstructions exceed the approved evidence ceiling?

When one Reviewer performs both functions at once, truth defects dominate the feedback and the creative brief becomes a growing list of prohibitions.

### 6. Subjective product claims were asserted without a cold test

Labels such as “natural aloud,” “retellable,” “strong forward motion,” or “different product type” were not supported by:

- a blind comparison;
- a read-aloud or listening test;
- an independent product editor;
- a forced rank against the FoC craft benchmark;
- a listener decision about whether they would continue.

These claims must be marked as unverified impressions unless an actual cold-review procedure supports them.

### 7. Material sufficiency was never treated as a possible blocker

The bounded notebook may support an accurate and vivid explanation of clay-envelope manufacture without supporting a complete 700–900 word narrative unit at the requested benchmark level.

The current material contains no documented ancient actor, witnessed event, surviving dialogue, decision, conflict, or local consequence attached to the envelope sequence. OIM A64678 supplies an excellent object and epistemic boundary, but a static object is not automatically a narrative engine.

The Reviewer should have tested whether the selected material unit could bear the product target. It instead continued trying to manufacture story energy through prompt constraints. If the evidence ceiling cannot support the target passage, the correct output is a blocker and a request to change the material/section route—not another prompt patch.

## Required action before any Writer Round 5

Do **not** prepare or execute another Writer round from the current task.

The Review Agent must first produce an experiment retrospective that audits the reviewers rather than the Writer. The retrospective must separate:

- direct observations from the committed outputs;
- subjective product judgments;
- causal hypotheses about why an output changed;
- claims that remain unverified because execution or listener evidence is absent.

## Required blind product review

Perform a product-only cold review before another truth-led diagnosis.

### Review packet

Include anonymized versions of:

- Writer Rounds 1–4;
- the Historical Substrate control if useful;
- one or more appropriately bounded FoC reference passages for absolute craft orientation.

The product evaluator may receive the approved product brief and benchmark boundaries. It must not receive:

- round numbers or chronological order;
- previous feedback or reviewer hypotheses;
- Writer-task prompts or composition decisions;
- truth-failure annotations;
- execution metadata that reveals which output is expected to improve.

FoC remains a craft benchmark, not a factual authority or wording template.

### Questions the product evaluator must answer

For each passage:

1. What promise is created in the first minute?
2. What narrative or investigative question pulls the listener forward?
3. What changes beyond the physical state of an object?
4. Where do stakes, consequences, uncertainty, or scale deepen?
5. What carrier persists through the passage?
6. What can a listener retell after hearing it once?
7. Does the passage feel primarily like story, investigation, explanation, catalogue, or museum interpretation?
8. Would the evaluator voluntarily continue listening? Why or why not?

The evaluator must force-rank the passages and independently mark whether each one clears the absolute product bar. “Best in batch” is not a pass condition.

## Separate historical-integrity review

Only after the blind product judgment is frozen should a separate truth auditor inspect claim and reconstruction compliance.

The truth audit must not rewrite the product verdict. Its job is to identify:

- unsupported claims;
- missing qualifications;
- invented motives or causal bridges;
- reconstruction beyond authority;
- specific repair constraints.

It should not infer that a truth repair will improve narrative quality.

## Decision the retrospective must surface to the owner

After the blind product review and separate truth audit, present one of these decisions:

1. **Material-route blocker:** the current envelope-only material unit cannot reasonably bear the required podcast passage; request approval to expand authority or choose a different P01 movement.
2. **Writer/objective blocker:** the material is sufficient, but the current Writer model/objective consistently defaults to explanatory prose; propose a bounded model or authorship-process test.
3. **Editorial-process blocker:** a viable draft exists, but clean one-pass resampling prevents convergence; propose revision by the same Writer using separate product-editor and truth-auditor notes.
4. **Evidence-backed continuation:** only if the blind review shows a passage genuinely near the absolute bar, define one narrowly justified next iteration.

Do not silently choose among these routes. The owner must approve the next production direction.
