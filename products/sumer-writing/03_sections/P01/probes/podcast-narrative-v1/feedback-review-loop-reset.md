## Reviewer self-audit

The previous review sequence made several mistakes.

### 1. Relative improvement was mistaken for convergence

The Reviewer repeatedly said one round was “stronger,” “promising,” “more narrative,” or “different in kind” because it improved against the immediately previous failure.

That was not enough.

The corrected absolute verdict is:

`ROUNDS_1_TO_4_ALL_FAIL_THE_HIGH_QUALITY_HISTORY_PODCAST_BAR`

A passage can be the best of the batch and still be far from publishable product quality.

Future reviews must report **absolute product status first** and relative rank second.

### 2. Editorial hypotheses were promoted too quickly into Writer constraints

The loop accumulated hypotheses:

```text
rich material
→ Writer-owned composition
→ operation-level movement
→ one material practice
→ attested carrier
```

Each hypothesis was treated as partially validated because the next output changed in the expected local direction. That was too strong a causal inference.

The experiments changed multiple variables, lacked blind product evaluation, and did not establish listener preference against the benchmark.

Carrier, operation-level movement, or a narrow practice may still be useful editorial tools. They are not proven solutions and must not become permanent harness requirements from this evidence alone.

### 3. Proxy optimization displaced the actual listening experience

The loop increasingly optimized things that are easy to inspect:

- one-practice scope;
- physical verbs and state changes;
- empty `causal_bridges_claimed`;
- local truth qualifiers;
- material carrier presence;
- word-count compliance;
- retellable object operations.

These can diagnose failures. They do not define a compelling history podcast.

Round 4 is the clearest warning: the prompt became more compliant and the truth handling improved, yet the result still reads primarily as an educational material-process explainer with OIM A64678 introduced late as an example rather than functioning as a sustained narrative engine.

### 4. Product editing and truth auditing were collapsed into one role

The Reviewer allowed valid truth defects to dominate each iteration, then assumed the truth repair plus one new composition constraint would also improve the listening product.

That assumption is no longer allowed.

The two evaluations must be separated:

```text
PRODUCT: Is this compelling to listen to?
TRUTH: Is every claim/reconstruction inside authority?
```

Truth repair may reduce product quality. Product improvement may create truth risk. Neither result implies the other.

### 5. Fresh-Writer resampling was treated as editorial convergence

The prior loop was not true editing:

```text
Reviewer diagnoses draft
→ diagnosis becomes new prompt constraint
→ fresh Writer creates a new draft without seeing the previous draft
```

This is useful for bounded experiments, but it does not let an author revise a promising passage in response to an editor.

For production quality, the system must be allowed to test a different process:

```text
draft
→ product edit
→ truth audit
→ revision of that same draft by the same Writer role
→ cold product review
```

### 6. Material sufficiency was not treated as a first-class blocker

The envelope-only notebook supports accurate, concrete explanation of an important material practice. It does not contain a documented ancient person, witnessed event, conflict, decision, testimony, or local consequence attached to that practice.

The Reviewer repeatedly tried to manufacture narrative load from composition rules rather than asking whether this bounded unit can bear a 700–900 word passage at the requested benchmark.

From now on, `MATERIAL_ROUTE_BLOCKER` is an acceptable and potentially correct result.

## Absolute product bar

The product brief defines a 60–120 minute investigation of writing as a technology–institution, not a catalogue of artifacts or a sequence of miniature explanatory essays.

The approved benchmark permits learning these functions:

- a clear long-form causal macro arc;
- material/sensory anchors with provenance;
- movement between ordinary life, institution, and system-level historical change;
- human presence through evidence where available;
- uncertainty without destroying narrative flow;
- emotional weight generated by evidence rather than hyperbole.

For this P01 passage, product review must therefore ask whether the listener experiences more than a changing object state.

A strong passage should create, where the evidence permits it:

- a listener promise or compelling reason to stay;
- an investigative or narrative question with forward pull;
- stakes, consequence, uncertainty, scale, or historical implication that deepens as the passage proceeds;
- a carrier that persists rather than arriving as a late example;
- movement between concrete material reality and why that reality matters in a larger human/institutional world;
- an ending that opens the next movement instead of closing a miniature essay.

These are product outcomes, not a mandatory scene formula or beat sheet.

## Correct next loop: evaluation loop, not Writer Round 5

Do **not** create a new Writer prompt yet.

The next loop has three stages.

### Stage A — blind product-only review

Create anonymized review copies of:

- Writer Round 1;
- Writer Round 2;
- Writer Round 3;
- Writer Round 4;
- the Historical Substrate control if useful as a baseline;
- one or more bounded excerpts from the approved FoC craft benchmark for orientation only.

A clean Product Editor receives:

- the approved product brief;
- the benchmark boundaries;
- anonymized passages in randomized order.

The Product Editor must **not** receive:

- round numbers or chronological order;
- previous feedback;
- Writer prompts or composition decisions;
- truth annotations;
- expected winner information;
- repository architecture discussion.

The Product Editor judges listening quality only.

For each passage it must answer:

1. What promise is created in the opening minute?
2. What question or uncertainty pulls the listener forward?
3. What changes beyond the physical condition of an object?
4. Where do stakes, consequence, scale, or historical significance deepen?
5. What carrier persists through the passage?
6. Does the passage move between concrete reality and a larger human/institutional world?
7. What can a listener retell after one listen?
8. Does it feel mainly like story, investigation, explanation, catalogue, or museum interpretation?
9. Would the evaluator voluntarily continue listening? Why?
10. Absolute status: `PASS`, `NEAR_BAR`, or `FAIL`.

Only after assigning absolute status may the evaluator force-rank the passages.

“Best in batch” is never a pass condition.

### Stage B — separate truth audit

Freeze the product verdict before the truth auditor begins.

A separate Truth Auditor then checks the same candidate passages against the notebook/evidence authority for:

- unsupported factual claims;
- invented motives or causal bridges;
- overstrong functional claims;
- unsupported reconstruction;
- missing qualifications that materially change meaning.

The Truth Auditor must not rewrite, downgrade, or upgrade the product-quality verdict. It reports only factual risk and the minimum repair needed.

### Stage C — route decision

After A and B, the orchestrator must present one route recommendation.

#### Route 1 — `MATERIAL_ROUTE_BLOCKER`

Choose this if the blind Product Editor finds that the envelope-only unit lacks enough stakes, human consequence, uncertainty, scale movement, or documentary/historical carrier to sustain the required passage.

Do not ask the Writer to compensate with more rhetoric.

Recommend changing the P01 slice or expanding the authorized material territory before another prose attempt.

#### Route 2 — `WRITER_OBJECTIVE_BLOCKER`

Choose this if the Product Editor believes the material is sufficient but every Writer sample consistently turns it into explanatory prose.

Recommend a bounded test of a different Writer model/objective or a different authorship-role split. Do not patch the existing prompt with another list of prohibitions.

#### Route 3 — `EDITORIAL_PROCESS_BLOCKER`

Choose this if at least one passage has a salvageable product spine but clean one-pass resampling prevents convergence.

Use that exact draft as the revision base. Give the **same Writer role** two compact, separate notes:

1. a Product Editor note describing listener-level failures and desired outcomes;
2. a Truth Auditor note containing only factual corrections.

The Writer revises the existing passage rather than starting from zero.

#### Route 4 — `EVIDENCE_BACKED_CONTINUATION`

Choose this only if blind product review marks a passage `NEAR_BAR` and the truth audit shows bounded, repairable defects.

Any next iteration must then target only those frozen findings.
