## 3. KEY RETROSPECTIVE FINDINGS

### A. The Sandbox Trap vs. Benchmark Scale
Every single draft suffered from spatial and narrative claustrophobia. While the benchmark (*Fall of Civilizations*) establishes a majestic macro horizon—connecting clay to desert ruins, turbulent rivers, irrigation canals, and the massive urban pressure of thousands of strangers living together—our drafts remained trapped inside an isolated pottery workshop.

### B. The False Dilemma: Invented Causality vs. Academic Lecture
- When Writers were allowed free composition (Round 1), they defaulted to **inventing motives** (tamper prevention) and **teleological steps** (token -> envelope -> tablet).
- When forbidden from inventing causality, Writers over-corrected into **defensive museum lecturers** (Round 4) or **passive cataloguers** (Round 2), lecturing the audience about what *not* to believe rather than telling a compelling historical story.

### C. The Envelope-Only Slice Cannot Sustain a 700–900 Word Narrative
Confining the passage strictly to the physical mechanics of hollow envelopes (Rounds 3 & 4) proved to be an editorial failure. Without human stakes, institutional friction, or the wider transition to surface recording (tablets), the envelope alone lacks enough narrative propellant. Writers had to pad the word count with tactile ASMR or academic disclaimers.
