# P01 Podcast Narrative Probe v1 — Round 2 Feedback

Status: `prepare_round_3_single_variable_composition_change`

## Decision

Round 2 fixed the main truth failure from Round 1: the Writer no longer invented a causal story in which loose counters created a fraud/tampering problem that caused envelopes, and envelopes then caused numerical tablets.

The current anti-causal boundary is working. Keep it.

However, the passage lost narrative energy and drifted back toward explanatory mode. The main reason is not the truth rule itself. The composition still chose a center of gravity that spans **multiple historical practices** and asks the passage to explain a transition from counters/envelopes to numerical tablets.

When causal invention is forbidden, that composition naturally collapses into:

```text
practice A
→ explain material affordance A
→ coexistence/contrast
→ practice B
→ explain material affordance B
→ summarize the difference
```

That is safer, but still too close to a historical explainer rather than an engaging podcast passage.

## Freeze everything except composition scope

For Round 3, do **not** change:

- repository architecture;
- Historical Substrate contracts;
- `writer-notebook.md`;
- editorial angle;
- truth boundaries;
- causal-bridge rule;
- target language;
- broad word range;
- clean Writer isolation;
- one-pass/no-repair execution;
- evidence access policy;
- style rules or prose technique rules.

Do not add anti-essay instructions, hooks, beats, scene formulas, sensory quotas, protagonist requirements or new narrative methods.

The only intended experimental change is the Writer's **composition scope**.

## Round 3 hypothesis

The strongest forward motion available inside the approved evidence does not require a transition between historical media.

Instead, the Writer can follow a bounded sequence of **operations and state changes inside one practice**.

For example, the envelope practice already contains supported movement:

```text
moist clay + counters
→ counters placed inside
→ envelope closed
→ surface can receive seal/impressions
→ clay dries
→ counters are no longer directly visible
→ exterior may carry inspectable information about enclosed quantity
```

This sequence has temporal and physical progression without asserting why the practice was invented or that it developed into another medium.

This example is diagnostic only. It is not a required prose outline, required opening or required beat sequence. The Writer still owns the telling.

## Required composition change

Round 3 composition must choose a center of gravity that is primarily contained within **one practice, one material operation, or one evidence-supported local process**.

Do not choose as the primary movement:

- token → envelope → tablet;
- envelope → tablet;
- hidden quantity → surface recording as a cross-media developmental trajectory;
- any other composition whose forward motion depends on moving from historical practice A to historical practice B.

A second practice may appear briefly as contrast or as an opening toward the next unfinished passage, but it must not be the destination that gives this passage its narrative movement.

The composition decision should answer:

- What material situation are we entering?
- What physically happens within that bounded practice?
- What state is different by the time we leave?
- Where do we deliberately stop before needing another historical practice to create motion?

Keep `causal_bridges_claimed`. It should normally remain `[]` unless the notebook explicitly supports a historical causal relation.

## Product target

Round 3 must preserve the gains from Round 1 and Round 2:

- concrete physical action;
- natural spoken Vietnamese;
- listener can picture what hands, clay, surfaces and objects are doing;
- forward movement rather than catalogue structure;
- no research metadata in prose;
- no constant recitation of uncertainty;
- no invented motives or genealogies.

But the passage should no longer need explanatory signposts such as repeated statements of what the "shift" or "functional change" means in order to feel like it progresses.

The listener should feel that **something material is unfolding**, not that the narrator is comparing two categories.

## Truth precision to preserve in Round 3

Round 2 still contained some claims stronger than the notebook supports. Avoid strengthening these in the next draft:

- Do not state that every geometric counter shape represents a specific quantity/item mapping unless explicitly supported.
- Do not imply a universal one-to-one correspondence between every exterior impression and every enclosed counter.
- Do not convert a functional affordance into a universal purpose.
- Do not make co-occurrence evidence prove a developmental relationship.

These are precision constraints, not material that must be narrated.

## Evaluation gate

Round 3 succeeds only if all of the following are true:

1. **Truth:** no invented historical causal bridge or unsupported genealogy.
2. **Forward motion:** the listener can feel a material process/state change unfolding inside the chosen practice.
3. **Retellability:** the listener can say what happened during the passage without reducing it to a list of artifact categories.
4. **Natural aloud:** it sounds like authored historical podcast narration rather than a paper or museum explanation.
5. **Meaning emerges:** the narrator does not need to repeatedly announce the conceptual significance of each affordance.
6. **Scope discipline:** the passage stops before another practice is needed merely to manufacture narrative progression.

## Diagnostic interpretation after Round 3

If Round 3 preserves truth and produces substantially stronger narrative movement, then Writer-owned **operation-level composition** is likely the missing creative layer and should be considered for the canonical production path.

If Round 3 remains explanatory even when centered inside one evidence-supported process, do not immediately add more architecture or more anti-essay rules. Reassess the Writer objective/model behavior itself.
