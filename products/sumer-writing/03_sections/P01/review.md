# Review — P01 first draft

Verdict: changes requested. The draft is evidence-aware but does not yet work as spoken historical storytelling.

## ISSUE-01 — Critical — R5 narrative redesign

The section advances through the evidence pack: object description, provenance, definition, chronology, archive bias, attribution and world-first. These are qualifications, not a developing dramatic question. Acceptance: rebuild beats around one concrete problem, a reveal and a consequence; do not preserve the current paragraph order.

## ISSUE-02 — Critical — R5 fact selection

Most claims in the pack are narrated because they are available, not because P01 needs them. Acceptance: assign every claim a story role and limit the narrative spine to 1–5 claims; support is optional, guardrails remain mostly silent and irrelevant facts are omitted.

## ISSUE-03 — Major — R3 spoken Vietnamese

Terms and constructions such as corpus, horizon, relations, glottography/semasiography and translated academic abstractions make the governing idea difficult to follow. Acceptance: use ordinary Vietnamese first and make every paragraph paraphrasable in one plain sentence.

## ISSUE-04 — Major — R4 channel voice

The opening reads like a museum catalogue and the ending resolves into abstraction. Acceptance: use a concrete anchor to create tension, move between object and system, and let weight come from an evidence-backed human or institutional consequence.

## ISSUE-05 — Major — R2 evidence wording

The claim that recorded relations were separated from “một lần trao đổi trực tiếp” is more specific than the evidence, because the exact transaction is unknown. Acceptance: state only the durable informational capacity that the object supports.
