# P09 — Ngày truyền thống sống ngừng tự tái tạo

## Narrative job

Narrate multicausal contraction; distinguish last object, competence and material survival.

## Entry state

Khán giả expects one conquest or last tablet.

## Exit state

Khán giả sees uneven contraction and decisive break: clay remains, competent reproduction disappears.

## Question

System chết khi không ai viết, không ai dạy, hay tablet cuối bị chôn?

## Payoff

Endpoint is loss of institutional reproduction; 75 CE is latest-known evidence, not extinction date.

## Anchor requirements

- Latest-known astronomical tablet, 75 CE, discovery-sensitive.
- Contrast active competence with silent material survival.

## Bridge in

Adaptation works only while institutions pay training cost.

## Bridge out

Durable clay becomes archive no living reader can access.

## Boundary

Không select one sufficient cause or synchronized endpoint.

## Risk

Avoid false cinematic finality.
